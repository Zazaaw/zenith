=======================================

commit b97f521): camera capture, face detection and alignment, the recognition
pipeline, liveness checks, the encrypted face store, guided enrollment and Face Lab.

ArcFace model (ArcFace.mlpackage)
---------------------------------

The face recognition model is InsightFace's ArcFace "w600k_mbf" from the
"buffalo_s" model pack (https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface), converted
to Core ML by Glance's tools/convert_arcface.py. Zenith compiles it on the Mac
at first use.

InsightFace's code is MIT licensed, but its pretrained models, these weights
included, are provided for non-commercial research purposes only. Zenith is a
personal, non-commercial project; any commercial use or distribution of this
model needs InsightFace's permission or a differently licensed model.
