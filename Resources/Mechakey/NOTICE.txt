Zenith Mechakey: third-party notices
====================================

Mechakey's sound packs come from Mechvibes
(https://github.com/hainguyents13/mechvibes, soundpacks/keyboard), which
publishes them under its MIT License with "Mechvibes" as their author:

  Cherry MX Blue       cherrymx-blue-abs        CherryMX Blue - ABS keycaps
  Cherry MX Brown      cherrymx-brown-abs       CherryMX Brown - ABS keycaps
  Cherry MX Red        cherrymx-red-abs         CherryMX Red - ABS keycaps
  Cherry MX Black      cherrymx-black-abs       CherryMX Black - ABS keycaps
  Topre Purple Hybrid  topre-purple-hybrid-pbt  Topre Purple Hybrid - PBT keycaps
  EG Oreo              eg-oreo                  EG Oreo
  EG Crystal Purple    eg-crystal-purple        EG Crystal Purple

Each pack's audio was cut down to the clips its config.json uses and
re-encoded from Ogg Vorbis to AAC, and its slices moved to match, by
Tools/zenith-mechakey-packs.py. The key names and the press and release
slices per key follow Mechvibes' V2 sound pack format
(docs/soundpack-config-v2.md).
