# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report security issues privately, not in a public issue, pull request or discussion:

- **[Report a vulnerability privately](https://github.com/satuvisual/zenith/security/advisories/new)**

Include what you can: a description and its impact, steps to reproduce or a proof of
concept, and the Zenith version from Settings under About with your macOS version.

## What to expect

Zenith is a personal project maintained on a best-effort basis, so allow reasonable time
for a reply and a fix, and please wait for the fix before discussing the issue in public.

## Scope

Zenith runs locally. It has no update, feedback or sharing service of its own, and its
builds are signed locally rather than notarized. Reports that matter most are those that
could let the app's permissions or its privileged helpers (fan control, the closed-lid
sudoers rule) be misused.
